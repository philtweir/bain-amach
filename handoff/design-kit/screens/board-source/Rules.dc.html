Rules
Build compound verbs as lines of cards on the table. The first to empty their hand wins. On your turn, make one of these plays:
1 · A full line
A meaning card from your hand, a verb from the table or your hand, and every particle it needs from the tops of the river.
cuir
síos
ar
Describe
2 · A line through a particle
Lay your verb on one side of a particle already down and your meaning on the other. It gets a crossing mark.
cuir
suas
le
Put up with
éist
Listen to
3 · A lone verb
Lay one verb on the table, then draw 2 cards. Not allowed once both draw piles are empty.
4 · Swap a meaning card
Trade a meaning card from your hand for the face-up one.
