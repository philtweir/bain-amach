# Toast

Short feedback above the hand tray: a compound made, a refusal, or a race collision.

**Compound made:** player dot, the Irish in `answer` size 20, then the English meaning and who laid it. Stays 2.5s; announced to screen readers ("Siobhán lays *cuir suas le*, put up with").

**Not quite:** `ba-toast-soft`, ink not red, with the retry icon and one line naming the problem: a compound that doesn’t exist, or a real compound not on this card. Second line says the cost.

**Real errors only:** `ba-toast-alert` (red) for a failed save or update. Never for game play.

**Taken:** `ba-toast-quiet` with "Taken" and who got there first; the builder resets.

**Don't:** stack more than two toasts; a new one replaces the oldest.
