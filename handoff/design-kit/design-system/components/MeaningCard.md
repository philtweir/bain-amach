# MeaningCard

The larger card holding three English meanings with their Irish answers; it ends every line.

**Markup:** `<div class="ba-card ba-meaning">` with three `<div class="ba-m"><span class="ba-en">Describe</span><span class="ba-ga">cuir síos ar</span></div>` rows. Add `is-hidden` to each `ba-ga` when answers are hidden; the answer stays in the markup for screen readers only after the line is laid.

**Sizes:** hand, a wide card (`card-meaning-w` × `card-meaning-h`, 200 × 116) with English at 12px capitals and Irish at 16px; `ba-river` (the face-up pile, 60px square, one meaning); `ba-table` (64px square, only the meaning used, any rotation). Only the hand card is wide; table and print cards stay square.

**Spoken label:** "Meaning card: describe, *cuir síos ar*; put up with, *cuir suas le*; get from, *bain as*." With answers hidden, only the English.
